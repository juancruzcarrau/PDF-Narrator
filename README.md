# PDF Narrator

**Listen to your PDFs.** PDF Narrator reads the text of a PDF aloud using your Mac's built-in voices.
It's a small window that sits next to Preview (or whatever you read in), so you can follow along,
or just listen while you do something else.

It reads only the main text, not the clutter around it. Page numbers, headers and footers,
footnotes, captions, chart labels, references and copyright notices are skipped, so what you hear
sounds like someone reading the document to you.

## Download

**[⬇ Download PDF Narrator](https://github.com/juancruzcarrau/PDF-Narrator/releases/latest/download/PDF-Narrator.dmg)**

Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

## Install

1. Open the downloaded **PDF-Narrator.dmg** and drag **PDF Narrator** into the **Applications** folder.
2. The first time you open it, macOS blocks it, because the app isn't sold through Apple.
   This only happens once:
   - Double-click PDF Narrator. When macOS says it can't be opened, click **Done**.
   - Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway**
     next to the PDF Narrator message. Confirm with your password.

   After that it opens normally.

## How to use it

- **Open a PDF:** drop it on the window, or press ⌘O.
- **Start reading anywhere:** click any word in the text. The word being spoken is highlighted.
- **Play / pause:** the Space bar, your keyboard's play/pause key, or your AirPods.
- **Skip back or ahead a paragraph:** ⌘← and ⌘→, or the ⏪ ⏩ buttons.
- **Jump to a chapter:** if the PDF has a table of contents, the chapters appear in a sidebar.
  Click one to start reading there. The sidebar button next to ⋯ shows or hides it.
- **Reading time:** next to the progress bar you see how long the document takes to read,
  and once you start, how much time is left.
- **Speed:** use the slider between the 🐢 and the 🐇.
- **Read everything:** turn off **⋯ → Main text only** to hear headings, captions and footnotes too.
- **Keep it on top:** **⋯ → Keep window on top** keeps it above other windows while you read.

### Choosing a voice

Open **⋯ → Voice**. **System Voice** uses the voice chosen in
**System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content**, including the natural-sounding Siri voices.
You can download more and better voices there, under **System Voice → Manage Voices**.

## Updates

When a new version comes out, a bar saying **"Version … is available"** appears at the top of the window.
Click **Update**: the app downloads the new version, replaces itself and reopens. You can also check
any time from **⋯ → Check for Updates…**.

Keep the app in your Applications folder so it can update itself.

## Good to know

- PDF Narrator needs a PDF with real, selectable text. Scanned pages (photos of paper) can't be read.
- Everything happens on your Mac. Your PDFs are never uploaded anywhere.
